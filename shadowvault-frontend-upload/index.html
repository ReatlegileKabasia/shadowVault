PRIVACY & SAFETY

What ShadowVault does and does not protect

New entry details are encrypted in this browser before they are sent to the backend. The account username, entry title, timestamp, and encrypted details are stored by the service. The operator and infrastructure providers may process account, network, and operational data.

The service does not receive the local passcode as part of normal encryption. The browser stores a salted passcode verifier locally. Anyone with control of your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access your account or records. Entries created before encryption was enabled may be stored as readable text.

Safety limits: ShadowVault is not an emergency service, does not monitor entries or send alerts, and cannot guarantee that saving or syncing will work. Quick exit only closes the vault view; it does not erase browser history, device activity, notifications, screenshots, or records already seen by someone else. Test the app on your device and avoid using it where discovery could put you at risk.

Export creates a plaintext file on your device. Account deletion removes the account and its entries from the active application database after password confirmation; hosting-provider backups and operational records may be subject to separate retention. No retention period or deletion-from-backups guarantee is made here.

This in-app summary is not a complete privacy notice or a substitute for local emergency, legal, or professional support.
PERMANENT ACCOUNT ACTION

Delete account and entries?

This permanently deletes the account and its entries from the active application database. It cannot be undone. It does not guarantee immediate removal from hosting-provider backups, logs, or files you previously exported.
